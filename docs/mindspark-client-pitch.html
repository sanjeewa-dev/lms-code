A multi-tenant learning platform for institutes and tutors — classes, live teaching, assessments, payments, and AI tools in one product.

The challenge

Institutes juggle too many tools

Teaching, content, live classes, fees, and parent updates often live in separate apps — with little visibility into who is falling behind.

  • Materials shared as unprotected files that get forwarded freely
  • Payments handled outside the system, hard to track and approve
  • No early signal when a student is at risk
  • Parents left without a clear, trusted view of progress

The solution

Mindspark brings teaching, ops, and insight together

Each institute gets its own branded space — students, classes, payments, and data fully isolated — on a shared SaaS platform you can scale.

Teach

Classes, materials, live rooms, quizzes, assignments, and gradebooks in one hub.

Operate

Enrollment, attendance, fees, bank slips / PayHere, ID cards, and institute branding.

Improve

AI study tools for students, teacher copilot, at-risk signals, and a Parent Trust Center.

Built for everyone in the institute

Role-based portals that fit real workflows
